Plugins stay off until you turn them on. Each one has its own settings.

Soundboard plays a sound from a wheel, on the headset only.
Macros bind a key to a short sequence.
Performance draws FPS, processor, graphics, and memory as a thin line you can place and fade.
Autoclicker repeats one mouse button while Lumen is in the background.

Screenshots stay above a game without taking focus. Saved clips drop the frozen opening, and old clips can be selected and removed.
